Cherries: Good to Eat and Good for You Too!

Cherries are one of the most delicious and versatile foods around. Maybe that is the reason that an entire month is devoted to them. Did you know that February is National Cherry month? Grab a handful of these delicious treats while you learn a few facts about them. Be sure that you do not swallow the pit though!

There are many different types of cherries but they can basically be divided into two major groups: tart cherries and sweet cherries. Tart cherries are used for making pies. Sweet cherries are best for snacking. Tart cherries are said to have many health benefits. They are believed to relieve the pain of gout and arthritis. They are also loaded with Vitamin A.

In addition to being used for snacking and making pies, cherries are also perfect for being used in jams and jellies, baked into cookies and cakes, blended into creamy milkshakes, and of course being placed on top of a pile of creamy whipped cream on the top of an ice cream sundae! Cheers for cherries!
