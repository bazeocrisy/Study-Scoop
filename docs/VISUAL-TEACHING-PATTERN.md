# Reusable visual teaching pattern for Math chapters

This is the owner's approved Chapter 7 Lesson 7-1 pattern generalized to future chapters. Adapt it to the actual concept and existing architecture. It is an instructional contract, not a requirement to reuse fraction-specific code or an assertion of learning effectiveness.

## Author a complete reasoning sequence

1. Identify the thing being measured, counted or compared. Establish the whole, unit or reference before using symbols.
2. Show a concrete example before naming the rule. Keep one main idea per screen and use short, familiar sentences.
3. Demonstrate the action with genuinely changing mathematical pictures: partition, select, count, group, move, align or compare. Keep irrelevant dimensions stable so changes have a clear meaning. When reasoning refers to two figures, show both with consistent scale and clear captions.
4. Connect the visible evidence to the notation, one component at a time. Teach new representations and vocabulary before testing them.
5. Include a contrasting non-example that makes the important difference visible. Explain the boundary of the rule rather than only repeating it.
6. Progress from guided examples to independent practice and fresh assessment. Preserve the chapter's verified curriculum goals and required lesson structure.

## Every answer must teach

- Correct practice answers immediately show WHY with the relevant picture. A congratulation or restated answer is insufficient.
- Each wrong choice has its own observable correction and next reasoning action. Do not guess the child's thinking from an ambiguous response; say what the response counts or conflicts with.
- Typed questions cover meaningful error classes and boundaries, including blank/invalid input separately from mathematical mistakes. Enumerate the probes and their actual rendered outcomes.
- Help starts with a useful observation, then demonstrates the next step, then provides a full worked example if needed. Record hints, counting scaffolds and revealed answers separately from unassisted first attempts, including after retries.
- Put the active explanation and supporting picture together on a phone. Check real pointer and keyboard activation, focus, scrolling, retry and revisiting steps; static HTML inspection cannot establish these interactions.
- Keep tests free of answer-bearing help until submission. Afterwards give visual explanations for both correct and wrong responses. Do not remove the mathematical stimulus needed to solve the question.
- Written reasoning stays self/parent reviewed. Never infer conceptual mastery from keywords, length or grammar.

## Include chapter resources

Vocabulary cards teach with examples and non-examples while preserving exact teacher definitions and provenance. Vocabulary practice explains the distinction being tested; avoid distractors rejected merely by grammar. Chapter practice has the same useful feedback and honest assistance history as lessons. The printable guide includes worked visual examples, common mistakes and self-check answers. Cumulative tests use fresh mathematical tasks across the chapter and reveal teaching only during review.

## Sample, review, then expand

Independently review one complete sample and present it to the owner before bulk expansion. Record an explicit approval and scope; continue authorized expansion after approval. Review every remaining lesson and resource independently rather than treating sample approval as their review. Ask reviewers to identify what a confused child still cannot infer, with screen/item IDs and actual rendered evidence. Disclose content authorship so self-review does not become an independent verdict.

Keep technical correctness, independent teaching quality and observed child usability as three separate verdicts. Automated schema/model/path coverage is valuable evidence but cannot certify teaching semantics. Only an actual child observation can support a child-usability verdict; retention needs follow-up evidence. State unavailable audio, device, printing and assistive-technology checks plainly.

## Durable delivery

Preserve prior chapters, weekly content, privacy and memory-only behavior. Make authoring generators reproduce the reviewed content. Run repository and allowlist-built browser checks, register new checks in both CI paths, inspect phone-to-desktop screenshots, and verify deployed preview bytes. Publish a scoped evidence report with exact commits and rollback. Production still requires the owner's explicit approval of that release.
